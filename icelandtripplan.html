To do

    Good to know

    • Daylight runs roughly 7 hours at the start of February and about 10 by the end. The suggested plan keeps outdoor stops between about 9:30 and 17:30.
    • Troll.is can change the route for weather and road conditions. Multi-day tours can be changed or cancelled up to 24 hours before departure by email to info@troll.is.
    • The ice cave depends on weather and the glacier. Northern Lights are not guaranteed on the tour either.
    • Iceland's pools and lagoons ask everyone to shower without a swimsuit before entering. Sockets are the European two-pin type (230 V).
    • You have no car. Central Reykjavík is walkable. For the lagoons, Perlan and the airport, use buses, taxis or booked transfers.
    • If the weather turns, swap in the National Museum of Iceland (2 hours), Whales of Iceland (1.5 hours) or a local geothermal pool such as Sundhöllin.
